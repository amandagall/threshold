How It Works

Three letters. Each arrives when it's time.

Letter One
The Before
A few questions, written in the last days before you cross.
Letter Two
The During
Written in the strange middle place, when the vertigo hits.
Letter Three
The Arrival
Written when something shifts. Sealed for later.

Answer a question, and Threshold reflects back what it sees in you — not edits, not corrections, just a few honest sentences that say I see you. Once a letter is fully answered, it's filed and sealed. When all three are sealed, they're placed in your time capsule — to be opened on a date only you'll know is coming.
